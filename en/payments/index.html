Payment and Refund Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. Topping up

  • You top up through Kashier: bank card, mobile wallet, or another method shown on the payment page.
  • There may be a top-up fee. The amount and who pays it are shown before you pay.
  • Balances are in Egyptian pounds.
  • Student wallet: spent on Mada only; it cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to anyone.
  • Teacher wallet: top-ups and earnings are one balance, used for plans and code fees, or withdrawn (see the Teacher Terms).
  • Slower methods (such as paying at a Fawry outlet) are credited automatically as soon as Kashier confirms.

2. Buying

  • The price is shown before you buy, taken from the wallet, and the content opens at once.
  • Some courses have a duration and some videos a view limit; these are shown on the course page and are part of the purchase.
  • Every top-up and purchase has an invoice in your account.

3. Access codes

Some teachers sell codes outside the app. That arrangement and money are between you and the teacher; Mada is not part of it. A code opens the course once for one account, and Mada does not refund anything paid to the teacher.

4. No refunds

Content is digital and opens the moment you buy it, so all purchases and top-ups are final and not refunded, except:

  • Charged but not credited, or charged twice: we check and credit your wallet or refund the payment method.
  • The teacher removes you from a paid course: the price returns to your wallet automatically.
  • A fault on our side kept you from content you bought and was not fixed in reasonable time after you told us.
  • Any case where the law requires it, or where Mada decides on an exception.

Refunds go to your Mada wallet, except charge errors, which go back to the payment method. Access to a refunded course ends.

5. A problem with a payment?

Write to masaredu.app@gmail.com within 14 days, with the invoice or transaction number.

6. Bank disputes

If you dispute a payment with your bank before contacting us, we may suspend the account until it is resolved and deduct the reversed amount from your wallet.
