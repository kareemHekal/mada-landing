Content and Copyright Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. Content that is not allowed

You may not upload or post content that:

  • Is not yours and you have no permission to publish, such as someone else's books, explanations or videos.
  • Contains violence, hate, discrimination, or abuse of any person or religion.
  • Is sexual or unsuitable for students.
  • Contains someone's personal information without their permission.
  • Encourages cheating or leaks official exams.
  • Breaks Egyptian law.

2. Protecting teachers' content

Recording videos, filming them with a phone, sharing an account, or publishing content anywhere closes the account permanently with no balance returned, and the teacher who owns the rights may take legal action.

3. Report a violation

If you see content on Mada that takes your rights or breaks this policy, or Mada content published elsewhere without permission, write to masaredu.app@gmail.com with:

  • The link, or the course and teacher name.
  • What your right is and what proves it.
  • How to reach you.

We review every report and may remove the content and tell its owner.

4. Repeat violations

Accounts that repeat violations are closed permanently.
