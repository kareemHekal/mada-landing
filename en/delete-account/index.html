Account Deletion

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Students, parents and assistants: from the app

  • Open Mada and sign in.
  • Go to your account or settings page and choose "Delete account".
  • Enter your password to confirm.

Students: the wallet must be empty with no payment in progress. If you have a balance and want to delete, contact us at masaredu.app@gmail.com.

Teachers: by contacting us

Withdraw your available balance first, then write to masaredu.app@gmail.com from your registered email and we will close the account.

What is erased and what is kept

  • Erased at once: account details, photo, files, progress, messages, parent links and notification tokens.
  • Kept 5 years, then erased: financial records — invoices and transactions with name, email and phone — for accounting and fraud prevention.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Cannot sign in?

Write from your registered email to masaredu.app@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". We will confirm it is you and carry it out.
