Teacher Terms

Last updated: 4 October 2026

These apply to you as a teacher or teacher's assistant on Mada, alongside the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Payment Policy.

1. Your relationship with Mada

You are an independent teacher, not a Mada employee. You are responsible for your content, how you deal with your students, and any tax on your income.

2. Plans and commissions

  • Every teacher is on a plan that sets courses, students, storage, and Mada's commission on each kind of sale. Your plan is on the plan page.
  • Mada's commission is taken automatically from each sale; the rest goes to your balance.
  • A paid plan is monthly, paid in advance from the wallet, and not refunded once active.
  • With auto-renew on you get a reminder 3 days before the end and it renews from your balance. If the balance is short, auto-renew turns off and you move back to the free plan.
  • If we change prices or commissions we tell you at least 30 days before; a month you already paid is not affected.

3. Access codes

  • The code fee is taken from your balance when you make the codes, per code, by your plan. The amount is shown before you confirm.
  • If your balance is short, no codes are made; top up first.
  • The fee is not refunded, even if a code is never used or you delete it.
  • Selling codes happens between you and the student outside Mada, and is fully your responsibility.

4. Your balance and payouts

  • One balance: sale earnings after commission plus whatever you topped up. All of it can be withdrawn.
  • Earnings may be held briefly before becoming available, depending on platform settings.
  • Payouts go to a mobile wallet or bank account in your name, through Kashier, with a minimum per withdrawal shown in the app.
  • The transfer fee and who pays it are shown before you withdraw.
  • You are responsible for correct payout details; money sent to a wrong number you entered may not come back.
  • For your safety: your password is required before the wallet opens, and you are notified immediately when a payout method is added or changed.
  • If a student is refunded after the money became available to you, your share comes out of your balance, which may go negative until later sales cover it.
  • Mada may pause payouts while suspected fraud or a breach is reviewed.

5. Your content

  • You confirm the content you upload is yours or you have permission to publish it, and that it is lawful and takes no one's rights.
  • You let Mada store your content and show it to your students, and show your name, photo and course titles on your public page and in promoting Mada, while the content is on the platform.
  • You may delete your content at any time; students who bought it keep access until their subscription ends, or you refund them.
  • Mada may remove content that breaks the Content Policy.

6. Your students' data

Your students' data, including their phone numbers, is for teaching and follow-up inside Mada only. You may not copy, sell, share or use it for anything else.

7. Assistants

You may add assistants and set their permissions, including two sensitive ones: seeing students' phones and seeing money. You are responsible for everything your assistant does.

8. Closing your account

A teacher account is closed by writing to masaredu.app@gmail.com from your registered email, after withdrawing your available balance. If it is closed for a breach, Mada may keep money linked to the breach or owed to students.
